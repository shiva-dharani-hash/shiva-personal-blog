UI/UX • 2026

What I learned from designing my first UI/UX project

My first UI/UX projects taught me that good design is not only about making a screen look attractive. It is about understanding users, organizing information and making actions feel natural.

I started with user flows and wireframes before moving into high fidelity designs. Working through different iterations helped me understand the importance of spacing, typography, consistency and visual hierarchy.

CODING • 2026

My journey of learning web development

HTML gave me the structure, CSS helped me create visual hierarchy, and JavaScript showed me how a webpage can become interactive.

Instead of trying to learn everything at once, I found it more useful to build small projects and improve them step by step. Every project gives me another reason to understand a concept more deeply.

PROJECTS • 2026

Turning college ideas into practical projects

Academic projects are a great way to connect theory with practice. I enjoy taking a problem statement, breaking it into smaller parts, designing the solution and then implementing it.

My goal is to keep building projects that are simple enough to understand but meaningful enough to solve a real problem.
